**Elementec Brand | ChatGPT Images 2.0 Prompt Library**

Use this skill to generate any of the 6 Elementec visual campaign types using AI image generation (Wan2.7/DALL-E/ChatGPT Images). Each template is production-ready for client delivery.

## PROMPT TEMPLATES

### 1. FINANCE DASHBOARD
```
Take this bank statement and analyze all transactions from the last 30 days.
Create a premium-looking personal finance dashboard with a clean, modern design and dark theme.
Break all expenses into categories: Food & Dining, Travel, Fuel, Shopping, Subscriptions, Bills & Utilities, Rent/EMI, Investments, Entertainment, Miscellaneous.
Show: Total spent, Total saved, Biggest expense category, Most frequent expense, Hidden spending leaks, Recurring subscriptions, Weekend vs weekday patterns, UPI micro-spends, Top 10 transactions, "Where your money actually went" with percentages, Good vs bad spending comparison, Spending identity profile, Before/after savings potential, Suggested monthly budget, Goal tracker (phone/laptop/vacation/car/emergency fund).
Style: premium finance app dashboard, clean typography, minimal icons, modern charts, elegant spacing, subtle gradients, visually separated sections.
```

