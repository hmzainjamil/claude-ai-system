## USAGE
`/client-acquisition [mode] [niche] [target]`

**Modes:**
- `linkedin` — Generate LinkedIn outreach sequence (connect → follow-up → pitch)
- `cold-email` — Generate cold email campaign for target businesses
- `upwork` — Optimize Upwork profile + proposal strategy
- `fiverr` — Create Fiverr gig descriptions and pricing

## LINKEDIN OUTREACH SYSTEM

### Target Profiles
- Startup founders (US/UK/AU)
- Marketing managers at SMBs
- Agency owners
- E-commerce brand owners
- Search: `[niche] founder OR "marketing manager" OR "agency owner"` in LinkedIn

### 3-Message Sequence

**Message 1 — Connection Note (personalized):**
```
Hi [Name], I noticed [specific thing about their work/company] — really impressive.
I work with [their niche] businesses on [your service]. Would love to connect!
```

**Message 2 — Follow-up Day 3 (value-first):**
```
Hey [Name], thanks for connecting!
I checked your [website/profile/company] and noticed [specific observation about their problem].
I recently helped a similar business [specific result with numbers].
Happy to share how if useful?
```

**Message 3 — Pitch Day 7 (soft close):**
```
I'd love to offer you a free [audit/strategy call/sample] to show exactly what I can do for [their business].
No obligation — just want to demonstrate value first.
Interested in a quick 15-min call?
```

### Daily Target
- 10 personalized connection requests/day
- 10 × 30 days = 300 connections/month
- 5% response rate = 15 potential clients/month

## COLD EMAIL SYSTEM

### Lead Sources
- Google Maps → local businesses in target country
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator → decision makers
- Apollo MCP → `apollo_mixed_people_api_search` for targeted leads
- Vibe Prospecting MCP → `enrich-prospects` for verified emails

### Email Template (5-line formula)
```
Subject: Quick idea for [Company Name]

Hi [First Name],

[Line 1 — address their specific problem you noticed]
[Line 2-3 — your relevant experience + specific result with numbers]
[Line 4 — one specific free idea/suggestion for their business]
[Line 5 — portfolio link + "Happy to jump on a quick call this week?"]

[Your name]
[Your title] | [Your website]
```

### Volume Target
- 20 emails/day = 140/week
- 5-10% response rate = 7-14 replies/week
- Convert 20% of replies = 1-3 new clients/week

## UPWORK PROFILE FORMULA

### Headline: `[Niche] + [Result]`
Examples:
- "I help eCommerce brands 3X sales with AI-powered SEO"
- "I build AI automation agents that save businesses 20hrs/week"
- "Premium AI campaign visuals for luxury brands | 48hr delivery"

### Bio Structure
1. Problem you solve (1 sentence)
2. How you solve it (2 sentences)
3. Proof / results (numbers)
4. Call to action

### Rate Strategy
- Never below $15/hr as beginner
- Target $25-50/hr for AI services
- Move to $75-100/hr after 5-star reviews

## FIVERR GIG STRUCTURE

### Gig Title Formula: `I will [action] [specific deliverable] for [niche]`
Examples:
- "I will create a premium AI luxury brand moodboard and campaign visuals"
- "I will build a custom AI automation agent for your business"
- "I will design a premium finance dashboard from your bank statement"

### Pricing Tiers
| Package | Price | Deliverable |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Basic | $50-75 | 1 creative / simple deliverable |
| Standard | $150-200 | 3 creatives / full campaign |
| Premium | $300-500 | Full package + revisions + source files |
