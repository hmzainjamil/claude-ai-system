# proposal-writer — Winning Proposal Generator
**International Client Guide — Proposal Formula**

Generates personalized, conversion-optimized proposals for Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, and cold outreach.
NEVER generates generic "Dear Sir/Madam" proposals. Always hyper-personalized.

---

## USAGE
`/proposal-writer [platform] [job-description or brief]`

**Platforms:** `upwork` | `fiverr` | `linkedin` | `cold-email` | `agency`

---

## THE 5-LINE WINNING FORMULA

```
Line 1: Address the client's SPECIFIC problem (proves you read their post)
Line 2-3: Your relevant experience + SPECIFIC numbers/results
Line 4: ONE free idea/suggestion for their exact project (demonstrates value upfront)
Line 5: Portfolio link + timeline + soft CTA ("Happy to jump on a quick call?")
```

**NEVER use:**
- "Dear Sir/Madam"
- "I am a professional freelancer with X years experience"
- Generic copy-paste openers
- Long paragraphs without specific relevance

---

## UPWORK PROPOSAL TEMPLATE

```
Hi [First Name],

I noticed you need [specific thing from job post] — [one sentence showing you understand the exact problem].

I recently [specific relevant project] for [type of client] and [specific measurable result — e.g., "increased their conversion rate by 34%" or "delivered 3 campaign creatives in 48 hours"].

For your project specifically, I'd [one concrete free idea — e.g., "start with a competitor audit to find 3 quick wins before we touch your ad spend"].

Here's my portfolio: [link] — I can deliver [specific deliverable] within [timeline]. Open to a quick 15-min call to align on exactly what you need?

[Name]
```

## LINKEDIN PITCH MESSAGE

```
Hey [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about their business — problem or opportunity].

I recently helped [similar business type] [specific result with numbers] using [your service/approach].

I'd love to offer you a free [audit/sample/strategy call] — no pitch, just real value so you can see exactly how I work.

Worth a 20-min chat this week?
```

## COLD EMAIL TEMPLATE

```
Subject: [Specific observation about their business — 6 words max]

Hi [First Name],

[1 sentence: specific thing you noticed about their business that's a problem or opportunity]

I recently helped [similar company type] [specific result] by [your approach in one line].

One quick idea for you: [specific actionable suggestion for their exact situation — free value].

I'd love to show you what this could look like for [Company Name]. 15 minutes this week?

[Your name]
[Title] | [Website]
P.S. Here's a sample of my work: [link]
```

## AGENCY PROPOSAL (High-Ticket $500-2000)

```
[Client Name] — [Service] Proposal

WHAT I NOTICED
[2-3 sentences about their current situation and the specific gap/opportunity]

WHAT I PROPOSE
[Clear deliverable description — what they get, timeline, format]

WHY IT WORKS
[Reference to similar result you achieved — with numbers]

INVESTMENT
- Package: $[price]
- Timeline: [X days/weeks]
- Revisions: [number]
- Includes: [list of deliverables]

NEXT STEP
[Simple CTA — "Reply to this email" or "Book a 20-min call at [Calendly link]"]
```

## PROFILE HEADLINE FORMULAS

```
Formula A: "I help [target client] [achieve result] with [your method]"
Formula B: "[Niche deliverable] for [target industry] | [turnaround/key benefit]"
Formula C: "[Specific service] that [specific outcome] — [proof point]"

Examples:
"I help luxury brands create premium AI visuals that convert | 48hr delivery"
"AI automation agents for SMBs | Save 20hrs/week on repetitive tasks"
"Finance dashboards & campaign creatives for D2C brands | 5-star rated"
```

## RATE NEGOTIATION SCRIPTS

**When client asks for lower price:**
```
"I understand budget is a consideration. The price reflects [specific value/outcome], not just the time.
If budget is tight, I can offer a smaller starter package — [specific reduced scope at lower price] — so you can see the quality before we scale up. Would that work?"
```

**When asking for a raise after first project:**
```
"Working with you on [project] was great — [specific result you delivered].
For the next project, my rate is $[new rate] — this reflects [specific improvement/expanded scope].
I'm confident the ROI will be clear. Ready to get started?"
```
