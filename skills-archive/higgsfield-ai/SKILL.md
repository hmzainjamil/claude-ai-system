## 29 POWER PROMPTS — Complete Shot Library

### STRAIGHT LINE MOVES

**1 — Dolly In** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Slow drift through suspended debris toward the man. The floating glass shards in the foreground pass the camera, creating depth. Warm lighting, calm atmosphere despite the chaos.
```

**2 — Dolly Out** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Revealing the vastness of the destruction. As the camera retreats, more frozen debris enters the frame from the edges. The man becomes a small figure in a large, shattered hall.
```

**3 — Fast Dolly In / Rush** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
Aggressive, high-speed camera push-in toward the man's face. Heavy motion blur on the edges of the frame. The background tunnel-visions as the camera accelerates. Sudden, intense impact.
```

**4 — Pan Left** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Scanning the lobby from right to left. Revealing a wall of frozen fire and shattered marble columns. The light flares as the angle changes.
```

**5 — Pan Right** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Scanning from left to right, following the trajectory of the debris field. The frozen explosion stretches horizontally across the screen.
```

**6 — Tilt Up** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Vertical reveal starting at the polished marble floor covered in rubble, moving up the man's legs to his torso, ending on his face. Emphasizing his stature against the height of the lobby.
```

**7 — Tilt Down** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Vertical reveal starting from the high ceiling and shattered chandelier, panning down through layers of falling dust to the man standing below. Sense of vertical scale.
```

**8 — Dolly Left** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Lateral tracking shot. The foreground glass shards move quickly left, while the man in the mid-ground moves slowly. Strong parallax effect separating the layers of depth.
```

**9 — Dolly Right** `PRESET · 5s`
```
Lateral slide to the right. The background explosion shifts perspective relative to the man. The 3D space of the lobby is emphasized by the shifting debris.
```

**10 — Over the Shoulder** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
Camera positioned just behind and above the man's right shoulder. We see a fraction of his face and the full destruction field ahead. Anchored viewer perspective.
```

### ORBITAL + CRANE MOVES

**11 — Orbit 180** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
Smooth semi-circular orbital move around the man. The camera travels from his front view to his side profile. Background debris rotates in the opposite direction due to parallax. The light wraps around his face as the angle changes.
```

**12 — Full 360 Spin** `PRESET (Orbit Around) · 10s`
```
A complete revolution around the stationary man. The entire lobby spins around him. The frozen blast wave is visible from every possible angle—front, side, and back.
```

**13 — Slow Cinematic Arc** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
A gentle, curved camera track. Not a full circle, but a wide, sweeping arc that changes the composition from a flat shot to a dynamic ¾ angle. Elegant and controlled movement.
```

**14 — Jib Up / Crane Up** `PRESET (Jib Up) · 10s`
```
The camera lifts vertically into the air. The floor drops away, revealing the layout of the debris field from a high angle. The man looks smaller as the space expands.
```

**15 — Jib Down / Crane Down** `PRESET (Jib Down) · 10s`
```
The camera descends from the ceiling level, passing through a cloud of suspended dust and glass, landing at eye-level with the man. Immersion and settling.
```

**16 — Crane Overhead** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
Complex crane move. The camera starts low in front of the man, lifts up, and passes directly over the top of his head to end behind him. The floor perspective flips. Top-down view at the apex of the movement.
```

### ZOOM MOVES

**17 — Smooth Optical Zoom In** `PRESET (Zoom In) · 5s`
```
The camera stays fixed in space, but the lens magnifies the image. The champagne glass gets larger, filling the frame. No parallax, just pure magnification of detail.
```

**18 — Smooth Optical Zoom Out** `PRESET (Zoom Out) · 5s`
```
The camera stays fixed, but the lens widens. Starting tight on a single droplet, expanding to reveal the whole glass and the chaos surrounding it.
```

**19 — Crash Zoom** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
Snap zoom. Violent and instant magnification. The camera punches in from a wide shot to an extreme close-up of the glass in a fraction of a second. Whip-pan energy, shock factor.
```

**20 — Rack Focus** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
The shot is static. Start with focus locked on the champagne glass in the foreground (background blurry). Slowly, the focus pulls deeper, blurring the glass and sharpening the explosion in the background. Shift of attention.
```

**21 — Fisheye / Wide Angle** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
15mm fisheye lens simulation. The edges of the frame are curved and distorted. The glass looks bulbous and close, the lobby looks vast and spherical. Surreal, security-camera aesthetic.
```

### AERIAL MOVES

**22 — Drone Flyover** `PRESET (Drone Shot) · 10s`
```
Straight flight path over the top of the building. Looking down at the roof and the frozen debris cloud. The city horizon moves slowly in the distance.
```

**23 — Aerial Large-Scale Orbit** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
High-altitude orbital move circling the building at 200 meters. The surrounding city rotates as the building stays centered. Scale and context established. News broadcast framing.
```

**24 — FPV Drone** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
Fast, chaotic drone flight. The camera dives through a broken window, weaving between floating furniture and glass shards. Banked turns, motion blur, feeling of speed and danger.
```

**25 — Aerial Pullback** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
The camera starts close to the window explosion, then flies backward rapidly, revealing the whole building, then the street, then the city block. Infinite reveal.
```

**26 — Extreme Macro Zoom** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
Microscopic camera movement. Pushing into the surface of the glass shard until the texture of the reflection fills the entire screen. A world within a world.
```

### TRACKING MOVES

**27 — Leading Shot** `PRESET (Camera Follows) · 10s`
```
Camera moves backward at the same speed the man walks forward. He stays a constant size in the frame. The background recedes behind him. Classic 'walk and talk' framing.
```

**28 — Following Shot** `PRESET (Camera Follows) · 10s`
```
Camera moves forward behind the man, following him into the destruction. We see his back. We enter the scene with him.
```

**29 — Side Tracking** `CUSTOM · 10s`
```
Profile tracking shot. The camera moves parallel to the man as he walks. Foreground debris whips past the lens (fast), the man is sharp (medium speed), background is slow. Emphasizes lateral speed.
```

**30 — POV Walk** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
First Person perspective. Handheld camera movement, slight bob and weave mimicking human footsteps. Approaching the man. It feels like the viewer is walking up to him.
```

**31 — Through Shot** `CUSTOM · 5s`
```
The camera flies through a gap in the debris—literally passing through a hole in a shattered pane of glass. Transitioning from one space to another. Tight squeeze, revealing the man on the other side.
```

## Cinematic Image Prompt Formula

```
[SUBJECT] + [SETTING/ENVIRONMENT] + [CINEMATIC LIGHTING: motivated, directional, shadow/contrast] + [COMPOSITION: rule of thirds / leading lines / off-center] + [DEPTH: foreground element + midground subject + background detail] + [COLOR GRADE: warm/cool/teal-orange/monochrome]
```

**Example hero frame prompt (Nano Banana Pro):**
```
A man in a tailored dark suit standing in a luxury hotel lobby frozen mid-explosion.
Cinematic lighting from above-left casting deep shadows on his face.
Subject positioned left third of frame.
Foreground: shattered glass shards frozen in air.
Background: marble columns and frozen fire.
Teal and orange color grade. Photorealistic. No motion blur.
```

## Video Prompt Formula (Cinema Studio)

```
[CAMERA MOVEMENT] + [SUBJECT ACTION] + [ENVIRONMENT DETAIL] + [LIGHTING] + [AUDIO: ambient sound type OR "no dialogue"]
```

**Example (Kling 2.6):**
```
Slow dolly in toward the man. Character stands still, breathing slowly.
Frozen glass shards in foreground create parallax depth as camera moves.
Warm golden lighting from cracked windows. No dialogue.
```
